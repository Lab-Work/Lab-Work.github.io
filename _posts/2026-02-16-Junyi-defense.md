Dr. Junyi Ji successfully defended his PhD "Learning Multi-Scale Dynamics of Societal Cyber-Physical Systems: Applications to Freeways" on February 16, 2026. His work focused on the emergent dynamics of traffic waves in freeway systems, transforming massive trajectory data into scientific and practical insights for traffic management and congestion mitigation. Congratulations Junyi!
