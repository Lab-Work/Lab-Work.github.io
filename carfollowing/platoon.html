Car-following model

α — sensitivity 0.50 /s
τ — desired time gap 1.50 s
dΔxi/dt = vi-1 − vi
dvi/dt = α · [Δxi − (gmin + τ·vi)]
same α, τ for every follower i = 1…N

L∞ needs ατ² ≥ 4 (the stricter test)
