Following policy
Time gap: desired distance grows with speed. Space gap: desired distance is a fixed constant — one fewer parameter (no τ).
α — sensitivity 0.50 /s
τ — desired time gap 1.50 s
gdes — desired gap 20.0 m
A fixed following distance, independent of speed — much larger than gmin, which stays a separate, small safety floor.
dΔx/dt = v_lead − v_follower
